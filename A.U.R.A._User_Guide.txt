1. OVERVIEW & MISSION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.U.R.A. is an offline-first, tactical intelligence co-pilot designed for EVE 
Online capsuleers. Powered by an onboard Phi-3.5 Mini (3.8B Reasoning) neural 
engine, A.U.R.A. provides instant tactical analysis, directional scan fleet 
threat ranking, ship fitting reviews, vision OCR, and real-time intel radar 
monitoring with zero cloud latency or data transmission.


2. HARDWARE COMPUTE & NEURAL ACCELERATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A.U.R.A. automatically discovers and orchestrates host compute topology:
• NPU (Neural Processing Unit):
  - Intel AI Boost (OpenVINO Level Zero)
  - AMD Ryzen AI XDNA / XDNA 2 (DirectML & NPU Coprocessor)
• GPU (Graphics Processing Unit):
  - NVIDIA GeForce / RTX Dedicated GPUs
  - AMD Radeon RX & Radeon 680M/780M/890M iGPUs
  - Intel Arc Dedicated & Iris Xe / UHD Integrated Graphics
• Multi-Threaded CPU Vector Mesh:
  - Dynamically utilizes available physical cores and logical threads.

COMPUTE STRATEGIES:
• Default Mode (NPU-Exclusive): Zero gaming interference. Runs entirely on NPU
  leaving your GPU and CPU 100% free for high-framerate EVE client rendering.
• Non-NPU Fallback: Automatically routes workloads across GPU and CPU if no NPU 
  is present.
• Turbo Mode: Accessible via the top telemetry bar. Harnesses NPU + GPU + 
  all CPU threads for accelerated token generation.


3. COMBAT SYSTEMS & MODULES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ 🛰️ LIVE INTEL RADAR ]
• Automatically detects active EVE Online chat logs in:
  %USERPROFILE%\Documents\EVE\logs\Chatlogs
• Filters coalition and alliance channels by default:
  - The Imperium (*.imperium, delve, fareast, fountain)
  - Pandemic Horde (*.horde, fade, kalevala)
  - Fraternity & WinterCo (*.frt, *.winter)
  - The Initiative (*.init)
  - Brave Collective (*.brave), Snuffed Out (*.snuff), and military tags.
• Custom Keywords: Input arbitrary custom channel suffixes or words in the 
  keyword field to monitor specific corp or secret ops logs.
• Threat Scoring: Instantly flags Cynos, Bubbles, Capital Spikes (Titans, 
  Supercarriers, Dreadnoughts, FAX), and hostile scouts.
• Auto-Response Matrix: Optional toggle to automatically calculate instant combat 
  countermeasures upon detecting critical threats.

[ 📡 D-SCAN FLEET ANALYZER ]
• Paste raw in-game Directional Scan tables (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C from EVE client).
• Automatically parses hundreds of vessels in milliseconds (<3ms for 500 ships).
• Generates a tactical fleet breakdown by hull class (Capitals, Battleships, 
  Cruisers, Tacklers, Logistics, Interdictors).
• Calculates effective threat rankings and counter-engagement strategies.

[ 🛠️ FITTING LAB & DOCTRINE REVIEW ]
• Paste standard in-game EFT format fits (e.g., [Wolf, Solo Brawl]).
• Analyzes capacitor stability, active vs. buffer tank synergy, and engagement 
  envelopes.
• Strictly enforces hull-class module sizing rules (prevents hallucinations).
• Recommends role-specific tactical refinements for Brawling, Kiting, Fleet Tackle,
  FW Plexing, and Logistics.

[ 🖼️ RECON VISION & ATTACHMENTS ]
• Attach combat screenshots, killmail overviews, or tactical briefings.
• High-speed hardware OCR extracts ship names, modules, and combat text.
• Supported formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, BMP, WEBP, PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV.

[ ⏹ INTERACTIVE INFERENCE CONTROL ]
• Click the prominent '⏹ Stop' button at any time during neural generation 
  to immediately halt token generation.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
